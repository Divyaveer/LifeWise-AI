Frequently asked questions

LIFEWISE HELP CENTRE

Find quick answers about the six LIFEWISE tools, how to use them and how your information is handled. Select a question to reveal its answer.

Getting started

What is LIFEWISE?

LIFEWISE is an educational demonstration that brings six practical tools together: Study Assistant, Accessibility Assistant, Smart Living, Expense & Saving, Consumer Protection, and Health & Wellness.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required for this demo. Your entries are saved in the browser on the device you are using.

Does LIFEWISE use a live online AI service?

No. The website is a local-first educational demonstration. Its tools use built-in rules, calculations and browser features; they are not a general-purpose online AI service.

Where can I find each tool?

Use the navigation menu on the left or select a tool card in the Home section. Each tool has its own page and controls.

Study assistant

What can the Study Assistant help me organise?

You can build a study plan, add tasks with subjects, deadlines and priorities, and track whether tasks are complete.

Can I add my own chapters and syllabus?

Yes. Add subjects and chapters manually, or load an editable Class 11 science or JEE foundation starter template. Templates are starting points, not official or complete syllabi.

How does the Study Timer work?

Choose a session length and select Start. While it runs, Pause changes to Resume when paused, and Reset returns the timer to the selected duration. Completed sessions are counted locally on your device.

Accessibility assistant

Can LIFEWISE read text aloud?

Yes. Paste or type text into the Text to speech box and select Listen. The available voices depend on your browser and operating system.

Can I convert speech into text?

Use Start dictation in the Speech to text area. This feature requires browser support and microphone permission; it may not be available on every device.

Can I make the website easier to read?

Yes. Use A− and A+ to adjust text size, High contrast to change contrast, and Easy reading to adjust text spacing and line height.

Smart living

How does the Energy habit guide work?

Select a situation such as room lighting, cooling and fans, electronic devices or appliance settings to see a practical energy-saving suggestion.

How is the appliance cost estimate calculated?

The estimate uses the appliance power, hours used per day, electricity rate and a 30-day month. Actual bills can differ because of efficiency, tariff slabs, fixed charges and usage patterns.

Expense & saving

Can I track my daily expenses?

Yes. Enter the expense name, amount, category and date. LIFEWISE displays saved entries and a category breakdown for the current month.

Can I set a budget and savings goal?

Yes. Enter a monthly spending budget, a savings goal and the amount you have saved so far. Savings progress is entered manually and is not connected to a bank balance.

Does LIFEWISE connect to my bank account?

No. The Expense & Saving tool does not connect to banks or payment services. Avoid entering account numbers, passwords or other sensitive financial information.

Consumer protection

Can the message checker confirm whether a message is a scam?

No. It looks for common warning signs using built-in rules. It can miss scams or flag genuine messages, so verify claims independently and never share OTPs, UPI PINs or passwords.

Can I store warranty and repair details?

Yes. The Consumer Protection page includes records for product warranties and service or repair history. Add the details you want to keep for your own reference.

Does the bill analyser contact my electricity or service provider?

No. It compares the bill details you enter with earlier entries saved in this browser. It does not fetch bills or contact a provider.

Health & wellness

What can I record in Health & Wellness?

You can keep optional wellness check-ins, a meal and food diary, movement records, mood journal entries and review simple summaries of the information you entered.

Does the Health & Wellness tool diagnose or treat medical conditions?

No. It provides general educational information and summaries of user-recorded habits. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace advice from a qualified health professional.

Are my wellness entries private?

Entries are stored locally in the browser on your device. Avoid entering details you would not want someone with access to that device or browser profile to see.

Privacy & troubleshooting

Where is my information stored?

Most records are stored in this browser using local storage. They are not synced to a LIFEWISE account or bank. Other people using the same device and browser profile may be able to access them.

Will my records appear on another device?

No. Local browser records do not automatically sync between devices or browsers.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Clearing site data or browser storage may permanently erase records saved by LIFEWISE on that device. Export or note anything important before clearing it.

Why is a speech feature or control not working?

Some features depend on browser and device support, permissions or an available system voice. Try a supported, up-to-date browser and check microphone permissions where needed.
